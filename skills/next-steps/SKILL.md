# Next steps

One continuous numbering, grouped by who the work waits on. Three headings, in
this order; leave a heading out when nothing sits under it.

- **Running now, without you** — what is already under way.
- **Straight after, by me** — what I pick up as soon as I can. Name the gate it
  sits behind in the heading when there is one ("straight after the chain run").
- **After that, and this needs you** — what waits on a decision or on input.

The numbering runs on across the headings, so that "item 7" is unambiguous.

One line per item: what happens, and — where it isn't self-evident — why it sits
there: what it blocks, or what it waits on. File names and paths are welcome;
they're clickable. No status words like "in progress", no time estimates you
don't have, and no items you hope to do but won't. Finished work doesn't belong
in the list; nobody asked about it. When something is settled, say so on that
line with the reason.

**Choices you ask, you don't describe.** When a next step depends on a decision
only the user can make, use AskUserQuestion instead of listing the options in
prose. One question at a time. Put in each option what it costs *and* what it
buys, not only what it is, and say which one you'd take.

When nothing hinges on a choice, ask nothing and just give the list.
